# PixelMusic - Your Night. Our Frequency.

A beautifully simple, lossless music experience for late-night listeners, curious minds, and every song that pulls you a little further out. Built for Android.

## Features
- **3D Cosmic Saturn Particle Engine**: Real-time WebGL audio-reactive particle system with orbital velocity physics and customizable geometry modes.
- **Interactive 3D Hardware CAD Explorer**: 3D models for Planar Magnetic Transducers, Resonance Turntable, and Android 15 edge-to-edge hardware with manual orbit controls, exploded view, and wireframe analysis.
- **Web Audio Sound Synthesizer**: In-browser harmonic frequency audio engine with 3-band parametric EQ, subtle vinyl warmth generation, and audio analysis.
- **Parametric DSP Equalizer**: Presets for Late-Night Acoustic, Flat Reference, Vinyl Warmth, and Cosmic Spatial.
- **Synced Dynamic Lyrics**: Offline embedded LRC synced player interface.
- **Verified Releases**: Standalone APK distribution with SHA-256 integrity checksum verification.
